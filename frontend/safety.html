Safety Guidelines

Your safety is our top priority. Please follow these guidelines when using Lost & Found Portal For Campus.

Meeting Up Safely

Always arrange to meet in well-lit, public areas on campus, such as the library, student union, or campus security office. Never go to someone's private residence or an isolated location.

Verifying Ownership

If you found an item, do not post identifying details like ID numbers, credit card numbers, or exact contents of a wallet. When someone claims the item, ask them to describe these unique features to prove ownership before handing it over.

Personal Information

Do not share passwords, financial information, or unnecessary personal details. Only use the contact methods provided through the platform.

High-Value Items

For high-value items (laptops, phones, expensive jewelry), consider turning them in directly to the Campus Security or Administration office, and then create a "Found" report stating that the item is with security.
