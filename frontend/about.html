About Lost & Found Portal For Campus

Connecting students to reunite lost items with their rightful owners.

The Problem

Every semester, hundreds of items are lost on campus. From water bottles and notebooks to wallets and laptops. The traditional lost and found boxes are scattered across different departments, making it nearly impossible to track down missing items.

Our Solution

Lost & Found Portal For Campus centralizes the lost and found process into a single, digital platform. It allows students and faculty to easily report lost or found items, search a global campus database, and securely communicate to return items.

How It Works

Report

Submit a detailed report with photos of what you lost or found.

Verify

Admins review reports. Claimants verify ownership securely.

Reunite

Meet safely on campus to return the item to its owner.
